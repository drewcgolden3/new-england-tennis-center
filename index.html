About the club

Indoor courts, twenty minutes from anywhere.

The New England Tennis Center sits on Duval Road in Lancaster, with indoor courts open year round for members and non-members alike. It is a straightforward place to get a good match in, in February.

Between open court time, coaching, leagues and junior programs, there is a way in here whether you play twice a week or twice a month.
